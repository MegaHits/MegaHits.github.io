QUICK GUIDE

Editing the melody

  1. Select a tone channel in the left sidebar.
  2. Drag on the piano roll to create a note. Drag a note to move it; drag its edge to resize it.
  3. Right-click a selected note and choose Delete, or press Delete/Backspace.
  4. Preview both square-wave channels with Play, then export for your EEPROM.

In Select mode, Shift-click selects the notes between two points. Alt-click toggles one note; Alt-drag toggles every note crossed by the marquee.

Only MIDI pitches C2 through C6 can be encoded by MHS3. The roll is limited to that hardware range.
